# 🌱 How I Use This Garden

Welcome to my research garden.

This is a living collection of ideas, questions, readings, and reflections.

Unlike a traditional publication, this space is not a collection of finished arguments.

It is a place where ideas grow.

---

## 🌱 Seeds

Some notes begin as unfinished thoughts.

They may be:

- questions I am exploring
- ideas I want to understand better
- connections I have not fully developed

These are starting points.

---

## 🌿 Growing ideas

Some notes develop through reading, writing, and reflection.

They connect different concepts, scholars, cases, and experiences.

These ideas continue to change as my understanding develops.

---

## 🌳 Mature ideas

Some ideas become clearer over time.

They may become:

- research arguments
- publications
- new questions

But even mature ideas can continue to evolve.

---

## How this garden is organised

### Concepts

Ideas and theories that shape my thinking.

Examples:

- Legal pluralism
- Authority
- Recognition
- Governance

### Readings

Notes from scholars and literature that influence my work.

### Thinking

My own reflections, questions, and emerging arguments.

### Cases

Examples from environmental governance and conservation practice.

---

## Why a garden?

Research is not a straight line.

Ideas grow through connections, questions, revisions, and conversations.

This garden documents that process.
