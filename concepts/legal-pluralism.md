# Legal Pluralism

## Definition

Legal pluralism refers to the existence of multiple legal orders within the same social space.

## Why does it matter?

Environmental governance is rarely governed only by state law.

Different actors interact through:

- State regulations
- Customary law
- Community rules
- NGO procedures
- International standards

## My reflections

Legal pluralism helps explain why conservation implementation differs from official policies.

The question is not only whether multiple laws exist, but how different legal orders interact.
