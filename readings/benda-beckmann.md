# Franz von Benda-Beckmann — Legal Pluralism

## Main idea

Legal pluralism is not only about different legal systems existing together.

It is about how people experience, negotiate, and use different normative orders in everyday life.

## Important contribution

Benda-Beckmann emphasizes that law should be studied in practice.

People may move between different legal forums and normative systems depending on:

- context
- interests
- relationships
- power

## Connection to my thinking

This helps explain conservation governance because actors do not interact with one single legal framework.

Government rules, customary practices, project requirements, and community norms may overlap.

## Reflection

The existence of multiple legal orders does not automatically mean equality between them.

Some legal orders have greater recognition and authority than others.

How do communities navigate overlapping legal orders when these orders have unequal authority?
