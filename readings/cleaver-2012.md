# Frances Cleaver (2012) — Development Through Bricolage

## Where you are in the garden

→ Conversations with thinkers  
→ Legal Pluralism family

## Main idea

Institutions are not always designed perfectly.

People often create, adapt, and combine rules from what already exists.

## Connection to legal pluralism

Legal pluralism helps explain the existence of multiple rules.

Institutional bricolage helps explain how people work with those rules.

## Reflection

Governance is not only a system of rules.

It is also a process of negotiation and adaptation.
