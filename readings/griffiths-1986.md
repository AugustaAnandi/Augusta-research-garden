# John Griffiths (1986) — What is Legal Pluralism?

## Main idea

Legal pluralism refers to the presence of multiple legal orders within the same social field.

## Important contribution

Griffiths challenged the idea that law only comes from the state.

Law can also emerge from:

- communities
- social groups
- institutions
- customary practices

## Connection to my thinking

This helps explain why conservation governance cannot be understood only through formal government regulations.

## Reflection

Griffiths focuses on the existence of multiple legal orders.

Later approaches explore more about relationships, power, and interaction between these orders.
