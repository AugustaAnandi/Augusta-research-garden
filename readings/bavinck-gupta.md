# Bavinck & Gupta — Legal Pluralism and Governance

## Main idea

Legal pluralism is connected to struggles over access, authority, and governance.

Different legal orders shape who can make decisions and who can access resources.

## Important contribution

Legal arrangements are connected to:

- institutions
- power relations
- resource access
- environmental governance

## Connection to my thinking

In conservation landscapes, rules about forests and resources are not only technical regulations.

They involve questions of:

- whose knowledge counts
- whose rights are recognised
- who has authority

## Reflection

Legal pluralism helps reveal that governance is not only about creating rules.

It is about relationships between actors and the legitimacy of different forms of authority.
