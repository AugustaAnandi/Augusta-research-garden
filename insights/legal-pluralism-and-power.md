# Legal Pluralism and Power

## The question

If multiple legal orders exist, are they equal?

## Thinking through scholars

Legal pluralism shows that law is not only state law.

But different legal orders do not always have the same authority.

Some are formally recognised.
Some operate through legitimacy.
Some remain invisible.

## My reflection

The important question is not only:

"How many legal orders exist?"

but:

"Which legal orders are recognised, by whom, and with what consequences?"
